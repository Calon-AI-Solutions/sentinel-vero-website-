# Policies

Clear records are the point of Vero, so we hold ourselves to the same standard. Every policy below is written in plain English and dated with its last review.

Policies in display order:

1. Privacy policy (`/legal/privacy`): How Sentinel Vero collects, uses and protects personal information through our website and Vero.
2. Cookie policy (`/legal/cookies`): Which cookies our website uses, why, and how to change your choices.
3. UK Data Protection Addendum (`/legal/data-protection-addendum`): The terms under which we process personal data on behalf of our customers under UK GDPR.
4. UK list of subprocessors (`/legal/subprocessors`): The third parties that help us run Vero, what they do and where they process data.
5. Data security at Vero (`/legal/data-security`): How we protect your data across our infrastructure, application and team.
6. Support policy (`/legal/support`): How to reach us, when we are available, and how quickly we respond.
7. Enterprise SLA (`/legal/enterprise-sla`): The uptime commitment and service credits available to enterprise customers.
8. Product update policy (`/legal/product-updates`): How and when we release changes to Vero, and how we tell you about them.
9. Professional services policy (`/legal/professional-services`): How we scope, price and deliver onboarding, data migration, training and custom work.
10. Devices policy (`/legal/devices`): The browsers, phones and tablets Vero supports, and how to keep them secure.
11. Referral policy (`/legal/referral`): Full terms for our customer referral scheme.
