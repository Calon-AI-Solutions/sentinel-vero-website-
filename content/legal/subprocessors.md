# UK list of subprocessors

To provide Vero, we use a small number of third party companies that may process our customers' personal data. We call them subprocessors. This page lists each one, what it does, and where it processes data.

Each subprocessor is bound by a written contract that protects personal data to the standard in our [UK Data Protection Addendum](/legal/data-protection-addendum).

## Integrations you choose

Some services connect to Vero only when you choose to connect them, such as a vehicle telematics provider or an accounting system. Where you hold the contract with that provider, it is your processor, not ours, and it does not appear on this list.
