# Cookie policy

This policy explains the cookies used on the Vero website, run by Sentinel Vero Ltd. It should be read with our [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy).

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Some are needed for the site to work. Others help us understand how the site is used. Similar technologies such as local storage and tracking pixels are covered by this policy too, and we call them all cookies here.

## Your choices

When you first visit our website, we ask which types of cookie you are happy for us to use. Only strictly necessary cookies are set before you choose. You can accept all, reject all, or choose by category.

You can change your mind at any time using the **Cookie settings** link in the footer of every page. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings, although blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site working.

## Categories we use

**Strictly necessary.** These make the website work, keep it secure, and remember your cookie choices. They are always on, because the site cannot function without them.

We do not currently use analytics or marketing cookies. If we add them, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.

## Cookies on this website

| Cookie name | Provider | Category | What it does | How long it lasts |

| vero_consent | Vero | Strictly necessary | Remembers your cookie choices | 6 months |

## Cookies inside the Vero platform

When you sign in to Vero, we use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect your account. These are required to provide the service and are not used for analytics or advertising.

## Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the cookies on our website change. The date at the top shows when it was last reviewed.
