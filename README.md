The Problem

Section headline: Growth doesn't break businesses. Spreadsheets do.

Body: Nobody plans to run a growing company like this. It happens one hire at a time, one workaround at a time — until the day nobody, including you, actually knows what's going on. That's not a technology problem. That's a control problem.

(Design note: three short callout cards below, not a wall of text — keep each under 2 lines.)

Three types:

The Stack. Timesheets in one app. Job tracking in another. Pricing living in someone's head.

The Guess. Profit isn't measured — it's estimated, or patched together by pasting numbers into ChatGPT or Claude and hoping for a clean answer.

The Headcount Fix. Compliance gets complicated, so the answer is always "hire another person" — never "build a system that handles it."

Four ways it shows up:

More people, more chaos. Headcount goes up. So does the chasing.

Every quote is a guess. Pricing lives in someone's head, not in the system.

You find out last. By the time you see the problem, it's already cost you money.

The business runs on memory. Not process. And memory quits, gets sick, or leaves.
