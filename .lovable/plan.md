# Sentinel Vero website

## What I’ll build

- A complete landing page at `/` using the supplied copy, structured for fast scanning and demo conversion.
- Three supporting pages: `/proof`, `/platform`, and `/about`.
- A compact mobile header inspired by the supplied Uptick reference: prominent Sentinel Vero lockup, simple navigation, and a highly visible “Book a Discovery” action.
- Shared navigation and footer across all pages, with working links and mobile menu behaviour.

## Landing page structure

1. Outcome-led opening with eyebrow, headline, supporting copy, one discovery action, and the live operational figures.
2. The problem, including the three short failure patterns and four practical symptoms.
3. One connected evidence chain from customer through payroll.
4. Positioning around See, Verify, Surface, Improve.
5. Team-size fit and existing-system fit.
6. Included capabilities, with AI quoting, AI advisory, payroll, mileage, and field app called out clearly.
7. Product-screen showcase using polished interface-inspired panels until genuine screenshots are supplied.
8. Proof preview linking to the dedicated page.
9. Five-step implementation process.
10. Partnership and pricing explanation without published figures.
11. FAQ, omitting the unverified security claim.
12. Final discovery call-to-action.

## Supporting pages

- **Proof:** a credible case-study framework that avoids invented client results, clearly ready for verified evidence when supplied.
- **How to use the platform:** role- and workflow-led guidance following the Customer → Payroll evidence chain.
- **About us:** operational-control positioning, principles, partnership model, and brand story without inventing team biographies.

## Visual direction

- Use the brand book’s palette exactly: Night Control `#07191D`, Vero Mint `#21D3A6`, Warm Bone `#F3F2EC`, and System Steel `#96AAA9`.
- Manrope for display headings, Inter for body and interface copy, with mono styling reserved for operational figures.
- Calm, precise, high-contrast presentation with mint used sparingly as a meaningful control signal.
- Evidence-chain lines, restrained motion, sharp information hierarchy, and compact panels rather than generic SaaS decoration.
- Preserve accessibility and readability on the supplied mobile viewport and larger desktop screens.

## Content decisions

- Keep the supplied wording intact except for minor punctuation or line-break adjustments needed for readability.
- Use “Book a Discovery” consistently and keep all pricing behind that action.
- Do not publish a security/compliance claim until approved wording is provided.
- Do not invent customer logos, testimonials, statistics, people, or company history.

## Technical details

- Build with the existing TanStack routes and Tailwind design tokens.
- Define the complete visual system centrally, then use semantic styles throughout.
- Add unique page titles and social descriptions for every page.
- Validate navigation, mobile layout, desktop layout, and interactions in the live preview.
